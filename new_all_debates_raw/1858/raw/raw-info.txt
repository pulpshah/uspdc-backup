

1858 Debates


Illinois Senate Debates
Abraham Lincoln (R), Former United States Congressman (IL) andStephen A. Douglas (D), United States Senator (IL)




Dates:


August 21, August 27, September 15, September 18, October 7, October 13, October 15




Cities:


Seven Illinois congressional districts: Ottowa, Freeport, Jonesboro, Charleston, Galesburg, Quincy, Alton




Format:


Each debate lasted three hours; first candidate spoke for one hour; the second for one and a half hours; the first replying for a half hour. Candidates alternated going first.




Topics:


Primarily slavery and the Union

Video reenactment available from C-Span
