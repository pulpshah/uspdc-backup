General Election Presidential Debate

September 25, 1988




Location:


Wait Chapel, Wake Forest University




City:


Winston-Salem, NC




Time:


8:00 – 9:30 p.m. Eastern




Sponsor:


Commission on Presidential Debates




Moderator:


Jim Lehrer, PBS




Panelists:


John Mashek, Atlanta Constitution; Peter Jennings, ABC; Anne Groer, Orlando Sentinel




Viewership:


65.1 million (Data provided by Nielsen Media Research)




Format:


No opening statements; each candidate questioned in turn with two minutes to respond; one minute rebuttal; follow-up questions permitted by panelists; two minute closing statements.

Questions divided between foreign and domestic policy
